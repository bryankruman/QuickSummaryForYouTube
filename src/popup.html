Quick Summary for YouTube™

Instant AI summaries from YouTube's own “Ask”.

Uses YouTube's built-in “Ask” AI — an experimental feature currently limited to YouTube Premium members and still rolling out. If a video has no “Ask”, it can't be summarized.

Settings

How to use

  • Right-click any thumbnail → Quick Summary.
  • On a video, press Alt Shift S to summarize it.
  • Choose Ask a question to ask anything about a video.
